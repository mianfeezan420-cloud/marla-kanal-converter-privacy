Privacy Policy — Marla Kanal Converter

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Marla Kanal Converter ("the App") is developed by F-Tech Developers. This page explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal information. The App has:

  • No user accounts and no sign-in
  • No server or cloud database
  • No contact list, location, photo, or file access

All calculations, settings (including your Marla standard and language choice), and calculation history are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us. If you uninstall the App, this on-device data is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and share the following types of data, as described in Google's own policies:

  • Approximate location
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs (including the Android Advertising ID)

This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive or store this data ourselves. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy > Ads.

Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
